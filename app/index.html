Bangladesh in Numbers is an independent reading of published official statistics. Figures come from the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, Bangladesh Bank, the World Bank, the United Nations and the other publishers named beside each figure. Where two publishers disagree — as the census count and the UN population estimate do — the site says so rather than picking quietly.
